01 / CONTEXT

A little context.

I'm Swanit Anuran — a developer from Bengaluru with a computer science background and too many interests. I build with Python, Go, Kotlin and Dart, explore security tooling, and experiment with LangGraph agent pipelines.

I like going deep on systems: figuring out how an API actually behaves, where a web app leaks, how an automation pipeline should be wired, or what makes an audio plugin feel right.

Music has been the other constant — years of producing electronic music taught me more about structure, timing and taste than any textbook did.
